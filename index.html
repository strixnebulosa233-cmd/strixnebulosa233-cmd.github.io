About

Since enrolling at Beijing Jiaotong University in 2023, I have been pursuing the eight-year integrated bachelor's-to-Ph.D. program at Jeme Tienyow Honors College under the supervision of Prof. Yongqi Sun.

Research

Bringing together reinforcement learning and agentic AI, my current work explores how LLM-based agents can plan, learn, and operate reliably over extended tasks, with particular attention to persistent state, context and memory management, feedback-driven control, and multi-agent coordination.

  • Reinforcement Learning
  • LLM-based Agents
  • Long-Horizon Agent Systems
  • Context and Memory
  • Multi-Agent Systems
